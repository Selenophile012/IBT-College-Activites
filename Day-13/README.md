# Bole Coffee House

A fictional business profile card for Bole Coffee House in Addis Ababa.

## Business

Bole Coffee House is a fictional Ethiopian coffee shop located in Bole, Addis Ababa.

- Border

- Typography hierarchy
- HSL colors
